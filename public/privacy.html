2. The most important points in brief

  • SchoolBuddy works without an account. We do not use tracking or advertising, and we do not load external fonts or scripts.
  • Without an account, your study data (tests, homework, study plan, flashcards, quiz results, goals, chat history, settings) is stored only on your device in your browser’s storage and is not transferred to us.
  • If you choose to sign in, we store your study data on our server so that you can use it on several devices (see section 5). Your chat history with Buddy still stays on your device only.
  • Only when you use the AI features is your input transferred to our AI provider to generate the answer (see section 4).

3. Hosting and server log files

The app is hosted by manitu GmbH in Germany. When the app is accessed, the server processes technically necessary data (IP address, date and time, requested file, browser and operating system, referring page) in log files in order to provide the app securely and reliably. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The log files are rotated weekly and automatically deleted after three weeks at the latest. We have a data processing agreement with the hosting provider in accordance with Art. 28 GDPR.

4. AI features

When you ask Buddy a question, have a quiz or flashcards created, or take a photo of a task, our server sends your text or photo to our AI provider Anthropic, which uses an AI model to generate the answer. We also send your grade, the language of the app, the subject if you selected one, and, for the chat, the most recent messages so that Buddy knows the context. We do not pass your name or your IP address on to Anthropic: the request is made by our server, not by your device.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give before using the AI for the first time. If you are under 16, your parents’ consent is required (Art. 8 GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app’s settings.

Our contractual partner is Anthropic Ireland, Limited in Dublin (Ireland). Anthropic processes the data on our behalf (data processing in accordance with Art. 28 GDPR). The rules for this are set out in Anthropic’s data processing agreement, the Data Processing Addendum (DPA). It is part of Anthropic’s commercial terms of service.

Anthropic also processes the data in the USA and in other countries outside the EU. This involves the US company Anthropic, PBC and Anthropic’s service providers (subprocessors), for example the cloud providers Google Cloud, Amazon Web Services and Microsoft Azure. Transfers to these countries are governed by the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission (Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021, Module 2 or 3). These are contractual rules specified by the EU to protect personal data outside the EU. They are agreed in the DPA (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

What happens to the data at Anthropic:

  • No training: Anthropic may not use your inputs and Buddy’s answers to train its AI models. This is set out in Anthropic’s commercial terms of service.
  • Deletion after 30 days: Anthropic automatically deletes your inputs and Buddy’s answers within 30 days.
  • Exceptions: If Anthropic’s safety systems detect a possible violation of Anthropic’s usage policies, Anthropic may keep inputs and answers for up to 2 years and the results of this review (classification scores) for up to 7 years. Anthropic may also keep data for longer if the law requires it.

Anthropic explains more about how it handles your data in its Privacy Center.

We do not store your inputs to the AI (questions, texts, photos) on our server, not even with an account: your chat history stays on your device. Flashcards and quiz results that you save are part of your study data (see section 2). To prevent misuse and uncontrolled costs, we count the AI requests per day. For this, we store a random device ID and the IP address only as a non-reversible hash value together with a counter. This data is deleted after two days at the latest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Please do not include personal data in your questions and do not take photos of people.

5. Account (optional)

You can sign in with an existing Microsoft, Google or Apple account. To do this, you are redirected to the provider’s sign-in page, where the provider’s privacy policy applies. We receive an identifier that uniquely identifies your account with the provider and store it only as a non-reversible hash value.

We also request your name from the provider (for Microsoft and Google via the “profile” permission, for Apple via “name”) so that the app can suggest your first name during setup. Apple only shares the name the first time you sign in. Microsoft and Google also send other information from your profile, such as your surname, the link to your profile picture or, for Microsoft, your username. We only use your first name: our server passes it on to the app on your device once and does not store it. You decide whether to keep, change or delete it. We do not ask for your email address.

With an account, we store the following on our server at manitu in Germany: this hash value, the name of the provider, sign-in times and your study data from the app (as described in section 2, except the chat history, and including your first name if you entered it in the app). The data is transferred in encrypted form (HTTPS). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the function you requested). If you are under 16, please ask your parents first.

For signing in, we set two technically necessary cookies (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG): “sb_oauth” for the duration of the sign-in process (at most 15 minutes) and “sb_session”, which keeps you signed in (at most 180 days or until you sign out).

Under “Settings → Delete account” you can delete your account and all data stored on the server immediately and permanently. When you sign out, the data is removed from this device and stays in your account.

6. Reminders and calendar export

Your device creates notifications itself; there is no push server. The calendar file (.ics) is created on your device and is not transferred to us.

7. Storage period and deletion

Local data is stored until you delete it: in the app under “Settings → Delete everything” (with an account: “Sign out”), or by deleting the website data in your browser or uninstalling the app. Data in your account is stored until you delete the account.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw your consent. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
